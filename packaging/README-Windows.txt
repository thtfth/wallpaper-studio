Wallpaper Studio - Windows
==========================

Nothing to install. Python is already inside the app.

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it, such as Documents.
   Don't run it from inside the zip - Windows unpacks it to a temporary
   folder and your saved states and images would disappear.

2. Double-click WallpaperStudio.exe.
   A console window opens and your browser loads the app.

3. Closing that console window stops the server. Ctrl+C works too.


Where your files go
-------------------
Everything lives in this same folder:

  Wallpaper Backups\   your previous wallpapers, 10 newest kept
  Generated\           images you generate, 10 newest kept
  data\                settings and your 5 save states

Move or delete the folder and the whole app goes with it. Nothing is
written to your Documents, registry or anywhere else.


"Windows protected your PC"
---------------------------
SmartScreen shows this for any program without a paid code-signing
certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".


Changing where images are saved
-------------------------------
Use the Folders section on the right of the app. "Move both back into
the app folder" undoes it.
